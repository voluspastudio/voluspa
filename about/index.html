IV. ABOUT

The Researcher

SECTION IV
San Pedro, California

VP Studio is the independent practice of Sarah Peroni, a designer working at the meeting point of art, design and research on perception, attention, memory and emotion.

She has made work for the web since around 2000, came to design from a career as a professional dancer, and brings a working knowledge of accessibility (WCAG) to everything she builds. The studio’s premise is simple: let people notice themselves noticing.
